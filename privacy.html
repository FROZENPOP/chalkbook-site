CHALKBOOK

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version

Your climbing journal stays on your iPhone. Your sessions only leave it if you join Friends and choose to share one. We don’t sell data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites. A few features do use outside services, and they’re listed below.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Your sessions, climbs, attempts, notes, gyms, photos and videos. Your iPhone’s own backups may include them.
  • Apple Health data. If you turn on Health workout linking, Chalkbook reads the workouts, energy and heart rate you allow. It is used only on your device, is never sent to our servers, and is never used for advertising.
  • Backups you export. They are files you control.

Services we use

  • Apple and Google sign-in. You can use Chalkbook without an account. If you sign in, Apple or Google confirms who you are and handles that under its own privacy policy. Your name, email and account identifier are kept in your iPhone’s Keychain so the app can keep you signed in.
  • Apple Maps. When you search for a gym, what you type goes to Apple so it can suggest places.
  • Supabase. Our backend provider stores your Friends data, listed below, on servers in the United States.

Chalkbook has no advertising or analytics services, and it doesn’t use AI to process your data.

Friends (optional)

If you join Friends, we store the following so other climbers can find you and see what you share:

  • Your account identifier, and the email address and name from your sign-in provider. These are never shown to other climbers.
  • Your username, display name, and optional bio and profile photo.
  • Friend requests, friendships, blocks and reports.
  • Your friend suggestion settings and the suggestions you dismiss.
  • Sessions you choose to share: date, caption, attempts, sends, duration and any route photos you attach.
  • Reactions and comments you leave on sessions.

Any signed-in climber can see your username and display name. On a private account, your bio, photo and shared sessions are visible only to approved friends. Shared sessions are visible only to the audience you choose, and friends of your friends may see you as a suggestion unless you turn that off.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your Friends data until you delete it or your account. Route photos on shared sessions are the exception: we remove them from our servers after 30 days, or sooner once you have shared photos on 10 newer sessions or our photo storage is full. If storage is full and none of your older photos can be removed, a new session is shared without photos. The session stays shared, and your original photos stay on your iPhone.

  • Delete account (Settings) permanently deletes your account, Friends profile, connections, shared sessions, photos, reactions and comments from our servers. Your journal stays on your iPhone.
  • Delete social profile (Friends) removes your Friends presence but keeps you signed in.
  • Deleting a shared session in the app also removes it from Friends. If you’re offline, this finishes when you reconnect, and friends may still see it until then.
  • Deleting the app removes your journal from your iPhone.
  • Deleted data can stay in our provider’s routine backups until they expire.
  • Reports are kept for up to 12 months for safety, even if either account is deleted. A report includes the reason given, the usernames involved and a copy of any reported comment or caption.

Community safety

Everyone who joins Friends agrees to the community rules. We automatically block some objectionable words. You can report a comment, post or climber, or block any climber. We review reports and remove content and accounts that break the rules.

Children

Chalkbook is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or its release notes.

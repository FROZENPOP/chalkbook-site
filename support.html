Common questions

  • Where is my journal stored? On your iPhone. Export a backup any time from Settings.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. It removes your Friends profile, shared sessions, photos, reactions and comments from our servers. Your journal stays on your iPhone.
  • How do I report or block someone? Open their profile, post or comment and choose Report or Block.
  • Why did photos disappear from an old post? Route photos on shared posts are removed after 30 days. Your originals stay on your iPhone.
